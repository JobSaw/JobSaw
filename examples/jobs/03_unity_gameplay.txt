FICTIONAL TEST JOB: Unity Gameplay Developer
Company: PocketOrbit Games
Location: Remote, Europe

We are a small mobile puzzle-game studio seeking a developer who has shipped
Android games. Work with a game designer and artist on gameplay, UI, and releases.
Commercial or independently published game experience is welcome, even if older.

Responsibilities
- Implement puzzle mechanics and gameplay systems using Unity3D and C#.
- Develop responsive game UI and 2D physics behavior.
- Integrate AdMob monetization and test advertising flows.
- Prepare builds and releases using Google Play Console.
- Collaborate on art assets and simple models using Blender 3D.
- Use Git to review changes and document game systems.
- Own small features from design through programming, testing, and release.

Required qualifications
- At least two years of Unity3D and C# experience or equivalent shipped projects.
- Evidence of published mobile games and hands-on product ownership.
- Understanding of 2D physics, game UI, and mobile monetization.
- Ability to communicate with designers and artists and explain technical choices.

Preferred
- AdMob, Google Play Console, Blender 3D, and Unity certification.
- Independent development and measurable download/release outcomes.

Cloud infrastructure, e-commerce APIs, and React development are outside this role.
